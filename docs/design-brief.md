# REPASO AI — design brief

## Frame

- **Purpose:** prove that REPASO AI is a closed-loop quality intelligence layer, not another ticket manager.
- **Audience:** Metrovacesa operations, quality, post-sales and innovation decision-makers.
- **Tone:** calm authority, technical care, operational optimism.
- **Direction:** architectural editorial control room.
- **Memorable idea:** every incident leaves a visible trace that becomes a preventive action.

## Visual system

- Deep ink for operational surfaces, warm paper for evidence and reports, turquoise only for verified progress.
- Editorial serif for high-level claims; precise geometric sans for controls and data.
- Fine construction-grid texture, measured borders, asymmetric hero composition and dense but calm dashboards.
- Motion is limited to the guided-demo progression, timeline state changes and one hero reveal.
- No generic gradient hero, floating glass cards or decorative analytics.

## Accessibility

- WCAG-conscious contrast, visible focus, semantic controls, reduced-motion support and mobile-first layout.
- Status is never communicated by color alone.
